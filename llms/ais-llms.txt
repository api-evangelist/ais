## What this company is

AIS Capital Management is a registered investment manager, not a software vendor. Its products
are investment programs — a discretionary Multi-Asset Allocation Portfolio family and the
systematic Frontera programs — delivered as managed accounts, not as software. Instruments are
limited to liquid, major-exchange listings, and the firm describes its methods as transparent
rather than black-box.
